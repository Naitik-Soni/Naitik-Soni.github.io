Work in production

Document intelligence for complex scanned documents

Silver Touch Technologies, about a year of R&D and solution development

A client’s scanned documents run from 2 to 200 pages, and the data I needed sits in a different place on every one. It was being entered by hand, at 50–100 documents a day.

I benchmarked multiple OCR and LLM/VLM models. Retrieval-style approaches turned out to be a poor fit, because this is structured extraction from variable layouts, not search. I built a custom pipeline that combines DocTR for text accuracy with Docling for layout accuracy, then moved to a VLM for more accurate extraction of the target data.

60–70%fewer low-confidence OCR boxes after I added a VLM verification layer, at some extra compute cost
~1 yearof R&D from first benchmarks to the working solution
